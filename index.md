# Privacy Policy — Talkman

_Last updated: 30 June 2026_

Talkman is built to keep your recordings private. This policy explains what the
app does and does not do with your data.

## The short version

Talkman has no account, no server, and no analytics. Your recordings and
transcripts stay on your device unless **you** choose to share or upload them.

## What the app stores

- **Recordings.** Audio you record is saved on your device only.
- **Transcripts.** Transcription is performed **on your device**. Your audio is
  not sent to us or to any third party for transcription, and it is not required
  to have an internet connection.

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to your recordings, transcripts, or
any personal information. There is no Talkman account and no Talkman server.

## Microphone

Talkman uses the microphone only while you are actively recording, and only to
create the recording you asked for. Audio is never captured in the background.

## Optional: Dropbox

If you choose to connect a Dropbox account, recordings you explicitly upload (or
enable auto-upload for) are sent to **your own** Dropbox account. This is entirely
optional and under your control. That data is then handled under
[Dropbox's Privacy Policy](https://www.dropbox.com/privacy). You can disconnect
Dropbox at any time in Settings, which removes the stored authorization from your
device.

## Sharing

When you use the system Share feature to send a recording, the file goes wherever
you direct it (Messages, Mail, another app, etc.). That is initiated by you and
handled by the destination app.

## Children's privacy

Talkman does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including
children.
